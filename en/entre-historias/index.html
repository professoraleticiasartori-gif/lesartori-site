Volume I

Coppélia

The doll who seemed real

In a village full of flowers, Doctor Coppélius builds wind-up dolls. At his window, a young woman reads all day long without ever blinking. Swanilda uncovers the secret of the workshop and dances like a doll until she becomes a real ballerina.

In class: mechanical and flowing movement, mime, the ear of wheat, the key and the festival of the bells.

Volume II

Esmeralda

The golden tambourine

In the square in front of Notre-Dame Cathedral, Esmeralda dances with her tambourine and her little goat, Djali. She offers water to Quasimodo, and it is his kindness that makes the bells ring when the truth needs to come out.

In class: rhythm and stillness, dancing with a prop in hand, traveling across the square and the message that kindness comes back to those who give it.

Volume III

Napoli

The Blue Grotto and the tarantella party

On the docks of Naples, Teresina and the fisherman Gennaro set out by boat. A storm carries Teresina away to the Blue Grotto, and Gennaro heads back out to sea with a magic medallion to bring her home. The story ends with a big tarantella celebration.

In class: the slow, rolling sea, the quick and bouncy celebration, small jumps and the Bournonville style.

Volume IV

The Nutcracker

The most magical night of Christmas

On Christmas Eve, Clara is given a nutcracker doll. At midnight the tree grows, the toy soldiers battle the Mouse King, and the two travel through the snowy forest to the Land of Sweets.

In class: marching and waltzing, the snowflakes, turns and balance, and each sweet with its own way of dancing. Perfect for your holiday class.

Frequently asked questions

How do I receive the ebooks?

As soon as your payment is confirmed, Hotmart emails you access. The files are PDFs you can read on your phone or computer, or print.

Which version should I choose?

Baby Class is for ages 3 to 6, with one 50-minute class in 6 scenes. Pre-Ballet is for ages 7 and 8, with two classes a week, barre, exercises and choreography. If you teach both age groups, the Super Bundle includes both versions of all 4 ballets.

Can I print the coloring pages and the certificate?

Yes. The coloring pages, the certificate and the planning sheet were designed to print on letter or A4 paper and use with your students.

Can I share the ebook with other teachers?

Each ebook is licensed for individual use by the teacher who bought it. You're welcome to print the coloring pages and certificates for your own students as often as you like.

What if I don't like it?

You have a 15-day guarantee through Hotmart. If it's not for you, just request a refund directly on the platform.

Do I need to know the repertoire to use it?

No. Each ebook walks you through the story, the music and the ballet itself before the class, so you can teach with confidence.
